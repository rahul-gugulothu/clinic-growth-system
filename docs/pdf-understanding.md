# Original PDF — What It Actually Says

## Document identity

**Title:** Clinic Growth System — 30-Day Launch Plan → 90-Day Validation → ₹3–5L/month Target

It is described as a **Founder operating brief + intern execution manual**.

## Core thesis

Start as a **specialized, productized clinic-growth service**. Prove private clinics will pay for measurable improvements in discovery, enquiry handling, appointment conversion, reputation and retention. **Do not build software first.**

## Initial wedge

- Dermatology / aesthetic dermatology
- Mumbai
- Owner-led private clinics
- 1–2 paying pilot clinics by Day 30

If dermatology does not show enough commercial pain, the document says to test dental or IVF next.

## Business outcome

**Qualified enquiries → booked appointments → attended appointments → clinic revenue → retention → referrals.**

## Offer

The core offer has four phases:

1. **Diagnose:** search discovery, Google presence, website, reviews, enquiry process, WhatsApp, booking, follow-up, content and competitors.
2. **Fix:** website/landing-page improvements, Google Business Profile optimization, tracking, enquiry workflow, WhatsApp process, review-request process, content system and conversion improvements.
3. **Acquire:** where appropriate and compliant, use search, local discovery, educational content, social, partnerships and referral systems.
4. **Report:** qualified enquiries → booked → attended → revenue where reliable. Avoid vanity metrics as the main outcome.

## Week 1 research requirements

### 100-clinic database

The PDF explicitly asks for the first 100 Mumbai dermatology clinic records with:

- Clinic
- Doctor
- Specialty
- Area
- Phone
- Website
- Google rating
- Reviews
- Instagram
- Booking
- WhatsApp
- Visible advertising
- Content quality
- Obvious problem
- Priority

### 20-clinic mystery shopping

Contact 20 clinics as a prospective patient and record response speed, channel, follow-up, booking friction and professionalism. Do not misrepresent identity or create fake medical scenarios.

### 20-clinic website/Google audit

Audit 20 A-tier clinics for visibility, reviews, website/mobile, booking friction, service clarity, trust signals and compliance concerns.

## Week 2

Create the repeatable offer and supporting assets: website, audit template, outreach template, proposal, reporting template, CRM fields, compliance checklist and first 100 prospects.

## Week 3

The document defines operating quotas including 100 new qualified prospects, 50 personalized outreaches, 25 follow-ups, 10 conversations, 5 qualified calls, 3 audits and 2 proposals as weekly targets. These are operating targets, not guaranteed conversion rates.

## Week 4

Target 1–2 paying pilot clinics, run a 90-day pilot, and measure qualified enquiries, response time, booking rate, attendance, no-show rate and reliable revenue attribution where possible.

## Long-term company vision

The document says to think beyond "agency" toward **patient revenue infrastructure for independent clinics**: service → productized service → clinic growth operating system → software. Initially, people do the work and later software can automate repeatable parts.

## Decision gates

- **Day 7:** Do at least 20 clinics show a meaningful commercial problem?
- **Day 14:** Can qualified doctors be reached for conversations?
- **Day 21:** Will someone pay ₹50K+?
- **Day 30:** Can 1–2 clinics be closed?

If a gate fails, fix or change the model rather than spending more money blindly.
