# Clinic Growth System — V1 Scope

## Status

**Draft — not yet approved for implementation.**

The source PDF says not to build software first, while the current assignment from the mentor is to start a prototype. Therefore, this V1 is a **prototype scope**, not a claim that the business is ready for full production software.

## Prototype objective

Demonstrate one coherent growth loop rather than the entire long-term operating system:

**Lead/enquiry → qualification → appointment → follow-up → outcome → reporting.**

## Must-have prototype areas

1. Clinic overview / dashboard.
2. Prospect or clinic research record (for the internal workflow).
3. Leads / enquiries.
4. Lead details and activity timeline.
5. Conversations / WhatsApp workflow representation.
6. Appointments / calendar.
7. Basic patient lifecycle view.
8. Follow-up status.
9. Basic reporting / KPI dashboard.
10. Clinic audit view.

## Should-have after the core flow works

- Reviews.
- Referral workflow.
- Reactivation.
- Internal outreach pipeline.
- Proposal / onboarding workflow.

## Later

- Deep Practice Management System (PMS) integrations.
- Automated revenue attribution.
- Predictive models.
- Advanced multilingual AI.
- Network/referral effects.

The sequencing is informed by the MCP report, which marks deep PMS integration, advanced patient reactivation and revenue attribution as difficult areas.

## Explicitly out of scope for prototype

- Real patient medical records.
- Real patient diagnosis/history/photos.
- Production WhatsApp sending without proper authorization and compliance review.
- Medical advice generation.
- Medical-outcome guarantees.
- Full billing software.
- Replacing existing clinic practice-management software.

## Success criterion

A user unfamiliar with the project should be able to click through the prototype and understand:

**who the customer is → what problem is being solved → how an enquiry becomes an appointment → how follow-up happens → how the clinic sees the commercial result.**
