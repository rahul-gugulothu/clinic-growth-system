# Clinic Growth System — Market & Clinic Research

## Required research from the original PDF

### Competitor mapping

Find **30 agencies/companies serving Indian doctors/clinics** and capture niche, visible pricing, services, positioning, claims, case studies, social presence and weaknesses.

### Clinic database

Build the first **100 Mumbai dermatology clinic records** with the required fields listed in `data-model.md`.

### Mystery shopping

Contact **20 clinics as a prospective patient** and record response speed, channel, follow-up, booking friction and professionalism, without misrepresenting identity or inventing fake medical scenarios.

### Website / Google audit

Audit **20 A-tier clinics** for visibility, reviews, website/mobile, booking friction, service clarity, trust signals and compliance concerns.

## Research discipline

- Record the source for each important observation.
- Distinguish public facts from our own scoring or interpretation.
- Do not collect patient-level medical information.
- Do not invent missing values.
- Mark unknown fields as `Not found` or `Needs verification`.

## Current data strategy

Use publicly available business/clinic information for market research. Use authorized or synthetic data for clinic-internal operational workflows until an actual pilot clinic grants appropriate access.
